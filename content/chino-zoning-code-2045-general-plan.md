> *Reported from the Chino city council record of July 7 and 21, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Chino's city council adopted Ordinances 2026-008 and 2026-009 on July 21, amending Title 20 of the municipal code — the zoning code — and changing the zoning designations of a set of properties, both to implement the city's 2045 general plan.

The council introduced them on July 7 after a public hearing. Principal planner Michael Hitz and Andrew Hill of the consultancy Dyett & Bhatia presented.

The clerk's record of the July 21 discussion attributes the practical objections to a speaker it identifies only as Warren: the cost of an appeal, the noticing requirements, and how much the change slows staff-level review. The record then carries the city attorney's position — that bringing the zoning code in line with the general plan is a legal requirement. Council member Comstock asked how residents would be brought along, raising a workshop, an ad hoc committee or the IFS committee. The record notes a possible workshop in September.

When the two ordinances were introduced on July 7, the approval included an amendment to Ordinance 2026-008 on building heights.
