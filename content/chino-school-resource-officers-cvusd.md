> *Reported from the Chino city council record of July 21, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Chino's city council voted unanimously on July 21 to approve an agreement under which Chino Valley Unified School District pays the city for five full-time school resource officers.

The agreement is retroactive to July 1. The council increased the revenue the city expects to receive under it by $107,808.10, to an adjusted total of $1,125,712.43.

The money runs from the district to the city: Chino Valley Unified is buying police services from the city of Chino, and the city's budget records the payment as revenue.

Both items were on the consent calendar. Approving that block: Mayor Eunice M. Ulloa, Mayor Pro Tem Curtis Burton, Karen C. Comstock, Christopher Flores and Marc Lucio. Moved by Marc Lucio, seconded by Karen C. Comstock.

The council approved a second, unrelated agreement with the same district at the same meeting: $381,905.35 for school crossing guard services, which are staffed by civilians rather than sworn officers.
