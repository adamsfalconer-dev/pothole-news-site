> *Reported in July 2026 from a state filing made on May 11, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Pomona has proposed a 3.5-mile off-road bike and pedestrian path along a segment of San Jose Creek, between Temple Avenue and Murchison Avenue, and circulated a mitigated negative declaration for it on May 11.

The city's filing puts the route in the top priority band of Pomona's Active Transportation Plan, and describes it as "envisioned to provide pedestrians and bicyclists a safer off-street path of travel between community destinations and amenities."

Pomona is the lead agency on the filing as well as the proponent: the city is reviewing its own project, so there is no private applicant on the other side of the decision.
