The water and sewer increases Long Beach mailed protest notices about this summer are now adopted. The City Council voted 8-1 at its Sept. 8 budget hearing to approve the utility board's water and sewer rate resolution, according to draft minutes of the meeting. The increases, as noticed to ratepayers, are 6 percent on all water rates and 8 percent on all sewer rates. Second District Councilmember Cindy Allen cast the no vote.

The same 8-1 split adopted a 2 percent natural gas increase for residential customers, which the utilities department estimates at about 59 cents a month for the average home.

For water and sewer, the department puts the average single-family increase at $5.74 a month, $4.64 of it water and $1.10 sewer, about $69 a year.

The adoption closes the Proposition 218 sequence that began with notices mailed to ratepayers: blocking the increases at that stage would have taken a majority of affected account holders filing signed written protests before the Aug. 27 hearing closed, and the department's hearing materials named the Sept. 8 council vote as the step after that.
