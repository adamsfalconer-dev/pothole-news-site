> *Reported from the Chino city council record of June 2, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Chino's city council adopted Resolutions No. 2026-035 and 2026-036 on June 2, calling a general municipal election for Tuesday, November 3, 2026, for the election of certain city officers.

The same action asks San Bernardino County to consolidate the city's election with the statewide election that day — the standard arrangement, which puts city contests on the same ballot the county mails to every registered voter — and establishes the city's candidate statement regulations, which govern the statement a candidate may have printed in the sample ballot and who pays for it.

The item was on the consent calendar, and the vote was unanimous: Mayor Eunice M. Ulloa, Mayor Pro Tem Curtis Burton, Karen C. Comstock, Christopher Flores and Marc Lucio, moved by Marc Lucio and seconded by Curtis Burton.

Chino voters will also decide a city business licence tax measure the council placed on the same ballot on July 21.
