> *Reported in July 2026 from a city filing made on June 12, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Glendora's city council has adopted an amended Circulation Element for the city's general plan, and filed notice with the state on June 12.

The Circulation Element is the chapter of a general plan that sets the city's policy for streets, transit, bike routes and pedestrian networks. State law requires every California city to have one, and to keep it consistent with the rest of the plan.

The resolution the council adopted approves the general plan amendment and finds it exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act.

The exemption is CEQA Guidelines section 15061(b)(3), the "common sense" exemption, which applies where it can be seen with certainty that there is no possibility the activity may have a significant effect on the environment. The city's stated reason is that the update "constitutes a text update only."
