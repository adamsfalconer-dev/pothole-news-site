> *Reported in July 2026 from a state filing made on May 6, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

A developer has proposed 104 townhomes on a vacant site in Baldwin Park, and the city circulated a mitigated negative declaration for the project — the middle tier of environmental review, used when a project's effects can be reduced below significance — on May 6.

The project, Bassett Villas, is 20 two-storey buildings, each 27 feet tall, with private two-car garages. The filing also describes a leasing office and a recreation building with a pool and jacuzzi, a dog park, common open space, guest parking, and what the document calls a privately owned public park.

The filing says the project would not demolish any existing building on the site; the townhomes go on the vacant portion.

The state record lists the local approvals the project needs: a general plan amendment, a rezone and site plan approval.

The filing does not indicate whether any Bassett Villas unit would be deed-restricted.
