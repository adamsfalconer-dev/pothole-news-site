Seven candidates are on the Los Angeles County registrar's list for Norwalk-La Mirada Unified's four November 3 school board contests, and the only name in Trustee Area 5 is Mark Copland, the sitting trustee the board itself appointed this year. The seat that appointment filled is not Area 5. It is Area 6, and Area 6 is a contested race on the same ballot.

For Area 5, the registrar's list shows one name, and it belongs to a board member whose current seat is a different one. For a voter in Area 6, the list shows a choice between two candidates for the seat Copland occupies today, and one of them is Christopher Staples, one of the two applicants the board selected for interviews and passed over in choosing Copland.

The area arithmetic comes from the district's and the county's own records. The district's board page lists one trustee for each of Areas 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7. Its provisional board member, Copland, is listed with no area at all and a term of 2026, and the one area with no listed trustee is Area 6, the area the county's list shows as a live contest. The vacancy Copland was appointed to fill, created by the resignation of Dr. Lorena Vidaurre, is the Area 6 seat.

Copland, a teacher and parent by the ballot designation he filed, took out nomination papers for Area 5 on July 14 and filed them July 24, according to the county's list, which carries a last-updated stamp of Sunday afternoon. The Area 5 seat belongs today to Narcis Brasov, whose term runs through 2026, whose name appears nowhere on the registrar's list for this district, and whom the district's roster still seats. Nothing in the county's record says why any sitting trustee is or is not on it.

In Area 6, Staples, a parent and business owner by his designation, filed July 13. He faces Malaika Ware, who describes herself on the ballot as a mother and school counselor and filed July 30. Two of the three applicants for one appointed seat are now November candidates in two different trustee areas, and only one of them has an opponent.

Across the four contests, the registrar marks exactly one candidate as an incumbent: Norma Amezcua in Area 2, with an E for elected. She filed July 13 and faces Marybel Micaela Lorenzo, a mother, businesswoman and realtor by her designation, who filed August 4. Two of the seven candidates sit on the board today, Amezcua and Copland; Copland carries no incumbent mark for Area 5. In Area 4, the seat held by board president Dr. Rob Cancio, the candidates are Anthony Armendarez, a youth coordinator and parent, and Josue Alvarez, a business owner. Cancio's name, like Brasov's, appears nowhere on the list, and his term also runs through 2026.

The appointment itself ran through the spring. The board announced on February 23 that it would fill the Vidaurre vacancy, selected three applicants to interview on March 14, and named them on its March 23 agenda: Esthela Chavez-Prado, Mark Copland and Christopher Staples. The appointment was made under Education Code section 5091, the section the board's agenda item cited for the provisional appointment, and the oath of office was scheduled for the board's April 27 meeting, according to that meeting's agenda.

The county's candidate lists also trace this district's move to by-area voting. Norwalk-La Mirada board candidates, Vidaurre among them, filed for undifferentiated at-large seats in November 2022. Areas 1, 3 and 7 filed by trustee area in November 2024, and Areas 2, 4, 5 and 6 appear by area now. And a Norwalk or La Mirada parent is looking at a board that will seat at least two people new to it among November's winners: every candidate in Areas 4 and 6 is someone who does not sit on the board today.
