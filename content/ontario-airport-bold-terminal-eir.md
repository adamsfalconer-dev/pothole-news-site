> *Reported in July 2026 from a state filing made on May 8, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Ontario International Airport has begun the environmental review for a programme that would add a third passenger terminal.

The airport filed a notice of preparation of a draft environmental impact report on May 8 for what it calls ONT BOLD — Building Our Legacy and Destiny. A notice of preparation is the first formal step of a full EIR: it tells the state and the public that the study is starting and asks agencies what it should examine.

The programme, as described in the filing, is the construction of a new Terminal 3, modernisation of the existing Terminals 2 and 4, and optimisation of the airport's terminal roadway system, vehicle parking, rental car facilities and aircraft apron "to meet forecast aircraft fleet and passenger demand." Aging terminal utility systems and mechanical equipment would be replaced and consolidated, and select portions of airfield pavement addressed.

The filing lists 37 assessor's parcel numbers.

Ontario International is run by the Ontario International Airport Authority, a separate public agency from the city of Ontario.
