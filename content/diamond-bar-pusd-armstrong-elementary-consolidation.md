> *Reported in July 2026 from a district filing made on May 15, 2026, as part of our founding archive.*

Pomona Unified School District will close Armstrong Elementary School and move all of its students to Golden Springs Elementary, according to a notice the district filed with the state on May 15.

The filing puts the number at 215 students, as of the 2025-26 school year. Golden Springs enrolled 388 and has a capacity of 870, which is why no construction is needed: the district's filing states the consolidation "will not require physical improvements to Golden Springs ES, and no physical improvements, use, or development will occur at Armstrong ES."

The district's stated reason is enrollment. The consolidation, the notice says, "is proposed to address declining enrollment and ensure the efficient allocation of resources," and "will not increase enrollment in the District." The filing places the project in Diamond Bar, one of the cities Pomona Unified serves.

The change happens over summer 2026 and is in effect for the 2026-27 school year.

Because no physical change is involved, the district claimed a statutory exemption from environmental review under Public Resources Code section 21080.18, and said no categorical exemption was warranted for that reason.

Isela Vazquez, the district's chief facilities officer, is the contact named on the filing.
