**Pothole News covers local government across Los Angeles County — every city and every named community — and the Pomona Valley.**

We read the records: agendas, budgets, filings, the documents that decide what happens where you live. We don't cover the City of Los Angeles, which has a working press; we cover the places that mostly don't.

Most of the places we do cover share almost no dedicated coverage of their city halls, school boards, water districts, and county governance. Decisions about your taxes, your police, your land, and your schools get made in half-empty meeting rooms and posted — when they're posted at all — in PDFs nobody reads. We exist to read them, connect them across the county, and hand you what matters in a few minutes a week.

**[See exactly which places we cover, and which we don't yet](/coverage/).** That page is generated from our own coverage register, so it can't drift from what we are actually doing.

## What we do

- **Watch the meetings you don't have time to.** City councils, planning commissions, county boards, school boards, and special districts across our footprint — agendas, packets, votes, and the records behind them.
- **Follow the money and the land.** Contracts, budgets, campaign finance, and development — the recurring stories that only make sense when someone tracks them over time.
- **Keep the record.** When a decision happens and no one publishes the result, that gap *is* the story. We hold the standing record so accountability doesn't depend on who happened to be in the room.
- **Credit and connect other coverage.** When another outlet breaks it first, we say so, link them, and add what only a county-wide view can. See our weekly **Worth your click**.

## How we work

We're a small operation built to monitor far more public meetings and records than a newsroom our size otherwise could. Our sourcing, verification and corrections standards are on our [Ethics & Standards](/ethics/) page.
